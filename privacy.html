Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

LilyPDF ("unopdf.xyz") is a privacy-first PDF toolkit. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our website and tools.

1. No Upload of Your Files

All PDF processing (merge, compress, convert to image, split, annotate) runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never sent to our servers or any third-party server. We do not have access to your documents.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or process your PDF content, file names, or any personal data that you use within the tools. We do not require registration or account creation to use the service.

3. Information That May Be Collected

As with most websites, our hosting provider may collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and access times for security and operational purposes. We may use cookies or similar technologies only to the extent necessary for the site to function (e.g. preferences). We do not use tracking for advertising.

4. Third-Party Resources

Our site loads scripts and fonts from third-party CDNs (e.g. Tailwind, PDF.js, Google Fonts). Those providers have their own privacy policies. Your PDF files are not sent to these services; only standard page resources are loaded.

5. Your Rights

You may use our tools without providing personal information. If you are in a jurisdiction with data protection laws (e.g. GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete data. Because we do not store your PDF or personal data, there is nothing for us to return or delete regarding your documents.

6. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised when changes are made. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us through the contact options provided on our website (unopdf.xyz).
